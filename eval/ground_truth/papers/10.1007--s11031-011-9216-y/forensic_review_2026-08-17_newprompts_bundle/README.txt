Forensic review bundle
======================

Open viewer.html in any browser. It is self-contained -- no network,
no server, no install. Double-clicking it works.

  paper/     the original PDF and the text extracted from it
  analysis/  every file the review produced, including the raw logs
  figures/   extracted figures, if figure extraction was enabled

BEFORE FORWARDING THIS BUNDLE: viewer.html and analysis/report.md have
had local paths, session ids and run cost removed. The raw logs beside
them -- toolcalls.*.json, messages.*.json, report.json, run_meta.json --
are the unedited record and still contain all of it. They are kept
verbatim on purpose, because an audit trail that has been rewritten is
not one. Delete analysis/ if you are sending this outside your group.

This is an automated check of a paper's reported numbers and its internal record -- not a finding of misconduct. It is NOT adequate for definitive detection of wrongdoing or fraud.
